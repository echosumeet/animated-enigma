# Benchmark results

`scmplatform` 0.1.0. Generated by `PYTHONPATH=src python benchmarks/run_benchmarks.py`.

Synthetic panel: 40 SKUs x 540 days = 21,600 rows, seed 7. Daily demand is a multiplicative model (weekly and annual seasonality, linear trend, promotion lift, constant-elasticity price response) drawn from a Poisson distribution. A permanent +30% price step is applied to one third of the SKUs from day 405 onward to create genuine drift.
