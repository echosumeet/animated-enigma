## Data-generating process

- 8 products x 4 regions x 3 channels = 96 bottom series, 180 hierarchy nodes
- 260 weekly periods, seasonal cycle m = 52
- multiplicative product-family seasonality (amplitude 0.28) with a region-specific phase shift
- item-level trend, promotions on ~9% of weeks with lift up to 1.9x and a 20% post-promotion dip
- 12% of items introduced mid-history, 10% discontinued with a decay to zero
- integer demand, seed 20260815
- DemandPanel: 96 bottom series x 260 periods, 180 hierarchy nodes, total units 6,626,863, zero-cell share 36.4%

## Backtest configuration

Rolling origin, horizon 13 weeks, step 13, 4 windows, cut-offs at [208, 221, 234, 247]. Minimum training length 156 periods. Every model is re-fitted at every cut-off; MASE/RMSSE denominators and quadrant labels are recomputed from the training window only.

`hw_mul[m=52]` is reported for completeness, but 62 of the 96 series (65%) contain at least one zero week in the first training window and therefore cannot support a multiplicative seasonal model at all; those fits fall back to the additive form automatically.

## Hierarchical reconciliation

Base forecasts are produced independently at each of the 180 nodes (Holt-Winters additive where there are two full seasonal cycles of dense history, SES or SBA otherwise), then reconciled. WAPE by level, pooled over 2 windows of horizon 13. MinT uses a Schafer-Strimmer shrunk residual covariance; the fitted intensity on the last window was 0.428.
