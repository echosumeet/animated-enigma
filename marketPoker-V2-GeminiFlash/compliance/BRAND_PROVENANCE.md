# BRAND & ASSET PROVENANCE

## 1. Game Identity & Naming
- **Name:** Market Poker
- **Logo Mark:** Geometric stylized "M♠P" spade emblem rendered in CSS vector gradients.
- **Visual Aesthetic:** High-end Cyberpunk Dark Terminal meets Luxury Macau High-Stakes Felt.
- **Trade Dress:** Completely original interface design. No proprietary assets, logos, or typography from PokerStars, Kalshi, Polymarket, WSOP, or Robinhood are used.
