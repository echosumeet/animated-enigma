# Benchmark results

Generated by `benchmarks/run_benchmarks.py` (seed 7, 200 shipments = 600 documents).

Data-generating process: each shipment emits a commercial invoice, a packing list and a bill of lading from one ground-truth record, across three layout variants, eight vendors in eight countries, six currencies, all eleven Incoterms 2020 rules and three date formats. Noise is applied as three independent mechanisms: per-character OCR substitution at 0.006, field omission at 0.07 per printed field, and a real cross-document inconsistency in 18% of shipments.
