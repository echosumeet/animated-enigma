## Two-stage stochastic design: VSS and EVPI

12 equiprobable demand scenarios from the three-level shock model (national 20%, regional 22%, zone 18% standard deviation). Scenario totals span 168,044 to 356,804 units/period against a nominal 250,000. Unmet demand is priced at 45 per unit. 26 MILP solves in 12.6s.

| measure | expected cost/period | network |
|:---|---:|:---|
| WS - wait-and-see (perfect information) | 2,015,122 | varies by scenario |
| RP - stochastic here-and-now | 2,266,698 | DC00 DC03 DC05 |
| EEV - mean-value design, lived with | 2,325,011 | DC03 DC05 DC07 |

| headline | value | % of RP | reading |
|:---|---:|---:|:---|
| VSS = EEV - RP | 58,313 | 2.57% | what modelling the uncertainty is worth |
| EVPI = RP - WS | 251,576 | 11.10% | ceiling on any forecast investment |

Only 17% of the wait-and-see solutions choose the same DC set as the stochastic solution, which is the honest signal that the site decision is genuinely uncertain rather than merely uncertain in flow.
